# The Comeback — Personal Nutrition Plan

Companion to **The Comeback 5-Day** training plan in the app (Explore → Weekly plans).
Written for: male, 34, 6'4", starting at 330 lb, target ~220 lb in 18 months, hiatal hernia (unrepaired).

> General wellness guidance, not medical advice. Confirm the hernia-related items (and the barbell plan) with your GI doctor or a physical therapist. This plan assumes "no seeds" means whole seeds and seed-containing foods; if you also want **seed oils** out (canola, sunflower, soybean, grapeseed), the plan already cooks exclusively with olive oil, avocado oil and butter/ghee — just also label-check mayo and dressings.

---

## 1. The math

**Maintenance (TDEE)** — Mifflin-St Jeor at 330 lb / 193 cm / 34 y ≈ 2,540 kcal BMR × 1.45 activity ≈ **~3,700 kcal/day**. (The formula's error grows at BMI > 40 — treat this as a starting estimate and let the scale calibrate it.)

**Deficit** — 700–900 kcal/day ≈ 1.4–1.8 lb/week. Your goal (110 lb in 78 weeks) averages **1.41 lb/week — comfortably inside the safe 1–2 lb band**, and conservative as a % of bodyweight at your size. Expect it front-loaded: ~2 lb/week early, slowing as you shrink.

| Phase | Weight | Intake target |
|---|---|---|
| Months 1–6 | 330 → ~290 | **2,700–2,800 kcal** |
| Months 7–12 | 290 → ~255 | **2,400–2,600 kcal** |
| Months 13–18 | 255 → ~220 | **2,200–2,400 kcal** |

**Monthly calibration rule:** 4-week average loss under ~1 lb/week → cut ~150 kcal. Over ~2.5 lb/week for several weeks → add ~150 back. Take a 1–2 week maintenance break around months 6 and 12 — adherence, not physiology, is the real 18-month constraint.

**Protein: 190–200 g/day**, every day (≈0.9 g per lb of goal weight; ISSN-range for dieting lifters). Split into 4–5 feedings of 30–60 g. This is the single most important number on this page — it is what makes the weight you lose fat instead of muscle.

**Macro frame (phase 1):** protein 200 g · fat ~100 g · carbs 130–150 g. Lower-carb, not keto — the carbs fuel five cardio days and live around the lifting sessions.

## 2. The weekly rhythm

| Day | Eating | Training |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 4 meals + post-lift shake, ~2,750 kcal | Upper + 30 min elliptical |
| **Tue** | **Fast day — eat 9:00am–4:00pm**, ~2,500 kcal | 5-mile elliptical ~6:30pm (fasted) |
| **Wed** | 4 meals + shake, ~2,750 kcal | Lower + 30 min elliptical |
| **Thu** | **Fast day — eat 9:00am–4:00pm**, ~2,500 kcal | 5-mile elliptical ~6:30pm (fasted) |
| **Fri** | 4 meals + shake, ~2,750 kcal | Full body + 30 min elliptical |
| **Sat** | Normal eating | Full-body stretch (optional but do it) |
| **Sun** | Lowest-carb, lowest-calorie day, ~2,500 | Full rest / walk |

**Why the 5-milers sit on the fast days (keep this — it's a feature):**
- Evening training lands 2.5–3 h after your last meal → empty stomach → minimal reflux during cardio.
- Steady-state cardio tolerates fasting fine; fed days stay reserved for lifting, where feeding matters most.
- The 4pm cutoff means a completely empty stomach at bedtime — intermittent fasting has measurably *reduced* nighttime acid exposure in GERD studies. Your fast is hernia-friendly by construction.
- If the evening sessions ever feel flat, the fallback is doing the 5-miler at 7am before the 9am break-fast — not moving it to a fed day.

**Hernia timing rules:** never train within 2–3 h of a full meal; small snack 60–90 min out is fine. Last food ≥3 h before bed every night. Go easy near training on coffee, citrus, tomato sauce, chocolate, mint, carbonation and fried/fatty meals.

## 3. What to eat

**Emphasize (organic + produce first):** chicken, turkey, lean beef, pork loin, salmon/cod/tuna, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey; every leafy green, broccoli, cauliflower, zucchini, green beans, asparagus, peppers; berries, apples, melon; olive oil, avocado, butter/ghee; potatoes, sweet potatoes and rice in modest portions on lifting days.

**Out — corn in all forms:** corn tortillas, cornmeal, popcorn, corn syrup/HFCS (check sauces, dressings, "healthy" bars, some whey tubs).

**Out — seeds, including the sneaky carriers:** seeded/multigrain breads, everything bagels, granola, most protein and breakfast bars, whole-grain mustard, tahini and hummus, chia/flax added to yogurts and keto products, many crackers, poppy-seed dressings, bagged-salad toppers.

## 4. Example days

**Lifting day (Mon) — ~2,750 kcal, ~200 g protein**
- 7:30a — 4-egg omelet with spinach + peppers, 2 turkey sausage, berries *(~45 g P)*
- 12:00p — Big salad: 8 oz grilled chicken, olive-oil vinaigrette, avocado *(~55 g P)*
- 3:00p — Greek yogurt + blueberries *(~20 g P)*
- 5:30–7:00p — **train** (≥2.5 h after lunch)
- 7:15p — Whey shake *(~30 g P)*
- 8:00p — 10 oz salmon, roasted potatoes, asparagus *(~50 g P)* — done ≥3 h before bed

**Fast day (Tue) — window 9a–4p, ~2,500 kcal, ~180 g protein**
- 9:00a — 4 eggs + 4 oz smoked salmon or leftover meat, sautéed greens *(~50 g P)*
- 12:30p — 10 oz beef or chicken bowl: cauliflower rice, peppers, cheese, guacamole *(~60 g P)*
- 3:15p — **Final meal:** 8 oz chicken thighs or pork loin, large salad with olive oil, Greek yogurt + berries *(~60–70 g P — the yogurt/cheese digest slowly and stretch amino availability into the fast)*
- 4:00p — fast begins → 6:30p 5-miler → water + electrolytes only → bed empty

**Rest day (Sun) — ~2,500 kcal, lowest-carb:** eggs + avocado · tuna-salad lettuce wraps · steak + a big roasted-vegetable plate · cottage cheese in the evening.

## 5. Phase notes

- **Months 1–3:** build the habit and the 5-mile base (start at 2.5–3 mi, +0.5/week). Lifting at 2–3 reps in reserve, always — grinding reps mean breath-holding, and breath-holding is the one thing your hernia truly can't have.
- **Months 4–9:** progressive overload via the 2-for-2 rule; shift dumbbell lifts to the barbell as 35 lb caps out (same breathing standard). Strength will climb fast even in the deficit.
- **Months 10–18:** likely hernia-repair window once the weight is well down; plan a ~4–6 week light block around surgery, then the banned list (sit-up family, planks, heavy bracing) progressively unlocks. Visible abs at 6'4" plausibly means low-220s or leaner — treat them as the months 16–24 trophy, not a day-548 guarantee.
