# The Comeback — Personal Nutrition Plan

Companion to **The Comeback 5-Day** training plan and the **Comeback Plan** meal template in the app (Eat → Meal plan → Browse plans).
Written for: male, 34, 6'4", starting at 330 lb, target ~220 lb, hiatal hernia (unrepaired).

> General wellness guidance, not medical advice. Confirm the hernia-related items (and the barbell plan) with your GI doctor or a physical therapist — and at this calorie level, a check-in with your doctor about the deficit itself is worth it. This plan assumes "no seeds" means whole seeds and seed-containing foods; if you also want **seed oils** out (canola, sunflower, soybean, grapeseed), the plan already cooks exclusively with olive oil, avocado oil and butter/ghee — just also label-check mayo and dressings.

---

## 1. The math

**Maintenance (TDEE)** — Mifflin-St Jeor at 330 lb / 193 cm / 34 y ≈ 2,540 kcal BMR × 1.45 activity ≈ **~3,700 kcal/day**. (The formula's error grows at BMI > 40 — treat this as a starting estimate and let the scale calibrate it.)

**Intake: ~1,700 kcal/day**, held flat. Against a ~3,700 TDEE that is a ~2,000 kcal deficit — deliberately aggressive, chosen for speed. Two things make it workable rather than reckless: protein stays very high (§ below), and the deficit **shrinks on its own** as bodyweight and TDEE come down, so the plan self-tapers without ever changing the number you eat to.

| Phase | Weight | Intake | Expected loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Months 1–4 | 330 → ~285 | **~1,700 kcal** | ~2.5–3.5 lb/week |
| Months 5–10 | 285 → ~250 | **~1,700 kcal** | ~2–2.5 lb/week |
| Months 11+ | 250 → ~220 | **~1,700 kcal** | ~1.5–2 lb/week |

**Calibration rules — these matter more at this deficit:**
- Losing faster than ~3.5 lb/week after the first month, or lifts falling off a cliff, or you feel wrecked → **add ~200 kcal back** (a bigger dinner portion). Adherence and muscle retention beat the calendar.
- 4-week average under ~1.5 lb/week (unlikely for a long while at this size) → hold; the plan is already deep.
- Take a 1–2 week maintenance break (~2,300–2,500 kcal) around months 4 and 9 — a diet this deep needs scheduled air.

**Protein: ~190 g/day**, every day (≈0.9 g per lb of goal weight). At 1,700 kcal this is non-negotiable — it is the entire difference between losing fat and losing muscle. Split into 4–5 feedings of 30–60 g.

**Macro frame:** protein ~190 g · fat ~60–70 g · carbs ~90–100 g. Low-carb by arithmetic, not ideology — after 190 g of protein there simply isn't much room, and what's left lives around the lifting sessions.

## 2. The weekly rhythm

| Day | Eating | Training |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 3 meals + post-lift shake + snack, ~1,750 kcal | Upper + 30 min elliptical |
| **Tue** | **Fast day — eat 9:00am–4:00pm**, ~1,550 kcal | 5-mile elliptical ~6:30pm (fasted) |
| **Wed** | 3 meals + shake + snack, ~1,700 kcal | Lower + 30 min elliptical |
| **Thu** | **Fast day — eat 9:00am–4:00pm**, ~1,600 kcal | 5-mile elliptical ~6:30pm (fasted) |
| **Fri** | 3 meals + shake + snack, ~1,650 kcal | Full body + 30 min elliptical |
| **Sat** | Normal eating, ~1,750 kcal | Full-body stretch (optional but do it) |
| **Sun** | Lowest-carb day, ~1,650 kcal | Full rest / walk |

**Why the 5-milers sit on the fast days (keep this — it's a feature):**
- Evening training lands 2.5–3 h after your last meal → empty stomach → minimal reflux during cardio.
- Steady-state cardio tolerates fasting fine; fed days stay reserved for lifting, where feeding matters most.
- The 4pm cutoff means a completely empty stomach at bedtime — intermittent fasting has measurably *reduced* nighttime acid exposure in GERD studies. Your fast is hernia-friendly by construction.
- If the evening sessions ever feel flat — more likely at 1,700 than it was at 2,700 — the fallback is doing the 5-miler at 7am before the 9am break-fast, not moving it to a fed day. If both slots feel flat the same week, that's the "add 200 back" signal from §1.

**Hernia timing rules:** never train within 2–3 h of a full meal; small snack 60–90 min out is fine. Last food ≥3 h before bed every night. Go easy near training on coffee, citrus, tomato sauce, chocolate, mint, carbonation and fried/fatty meals.

## 3. What to eat

**Emphasize (organic + produce first):** chicken breast and thighs, turkey, lean beef, pork loin, salmon/cod/tuna/shrimp, eggs and egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, whey; every leafy green, broccoli, cauliflower, zucchini, green beans, asparagus, peppers; berries and apples; olive oil in **measured** amounts — at 1,700 kcal the fats are where the budget quietly disappears. Potatoes, sweet potatoes and rice now appear only occasionally, on lifting days.

**Out — corn in all forms:** corn tortillas, cornmeal, popcorn, corn syrup/HFCS (check sauces, dressings, "healthy" bars, some whey tubs).

**Out — seeds, including the sneaky carriers:** seeded/multigrain breads, everything bagels, granola, most protein and breakfast bars, whole-grain mustard, tahini and hummus, chia/flax added to yogurts and keto products, many crackers, poppy-seed dressings, bagged-salad toppers.

- 9:00a — Eggs + smoked salmon with sautéed greens *(~47 g P, 504 kcal)*

**Rest day (Sun) — ~1,640 kcal:** Greek yogurt berry bowl · chicken vegetable soup · Sunday steak with roast veg and cauliflower mash · cottage cheese with berries in the evening.

## 5. Phase notes

- **Months 1–3:** build the habit and the 5-mile base (start at 2.5–3 mi, +0.5/week). Lifting at 2–3 reps in reserve, always — grinding reps mean breath-holding, and breath-holding is the one thing your hernia truly can't have. Expect the scale to move fast; expect some flat gym days — that's the deficit, not weakness of character.
- **Months 4–9:** progressive overload via the 2-for-2 rule; shift dumbbell lifts to the barbell as 35 lb caps out (same breathing standard). Strength progress will be slower at this calorie level than it would have been at 2,700 — hold what you have and take PRs as bonuses.
- **Months 10+:** likely hernia-repair window once the weight is well down; plan a ~4–6 week light block around surgery (eat at maintenance those weeks — surgery and a 2,000 kcal deficit don't mix), then the banned list (sit-up family, planks, heavy bracing) progressively unlocks. Visible abs at 6'4" plausibly means low-220s or leaner.
